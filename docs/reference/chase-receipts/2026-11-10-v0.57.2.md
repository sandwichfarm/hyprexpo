## Consumer and publication boundary

A fresh upstream clone verifies the selected source is retained in maintained
master history and all nineteen plugin pins are ancestors. Genuine normal
no-revision HyprPM selection is checked after the replacement manifest lands,
before tagging; final selection and release-asset results belong in the pin PR
and publication notes. Nix-assisted consumer proof does not claim an unprepared
generic distro HyprPM installation.

The tag workflow builds the public Arch binary separately and attaches its
source/tag/VERSION, compositor ABI, dependency, Lua and artifact-hash provenance.
Its downloaded bytes and matching runtime/lifecycle must be verified separately
from earlier candidate binaries and workflow status.

Physical touchpad gestures, HDR hardware, rotated outputs and fractional-scale
hardware paths are outside this validation scope. The daily desktop installation
and its saved HyprPM registration are unchanged.
